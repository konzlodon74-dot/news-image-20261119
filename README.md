# The World in One Picture — Thursday, 19 November 2026

An entry for the make.fun bounty **"Sum up 19 November's world news in one
image"**.

`main.py` (Python, standard library only) validates the front-page image
`entry.png` against the bounty gate — PNG/JPEG, at most 1 MiB, at most
1024×1024 — and writes it to `/output/entry.png`. Run it with:

```
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.png
python3 main.py out.png    # or write somewhere else
```

## What's in the picture

One newspaper front page for **Thursday 19 November 2026**, six stories
across regions and fields:

- **Science — Voyager 1, one light-day from home.** NASA's confirmation
  event falls today; the probe launched in 1977 is about 16 billion miles
  away, so its radio signal now takes a full day to reach Earth.
- **US politics — Jeffries nominated for Speaker.** House Democrats picked
  Hakeem Jeffries 251–11; he is on track to become the first Black Speaker
  when the full House votes on 3 January.
- **Gaming — GTA VI launches worldwide.** After a 13-year wait, the global
  rollout begins and gamers enter Vice City.
- **Europe — EU proposes a €45 billion loan for Ukraine**, to be repaid
  once Russia pays reparations.
- **Asia weather — Cyclone Akvan nears India's east coast**, with landfall
  expected between Machilipatnam and Puri and red alerts in Andhra Pradesh
  and Odisha.
- **World — Islam on track to be the largest faith by 2035**, a Pew Research
  report projects, driven by higher birth rates.

Stories as reported on 19 November 2026 (in at least one time zone) by
Reuters, AP, the BBC, NASA/JPL, The Hindu and Pew Research coverage.
